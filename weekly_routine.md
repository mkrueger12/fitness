## SUPERSET EXECUTION GUIDE

**How to perform a superset:**
1. Complete exercise 1 (e.g., B1. Dips × 10 reps)
2. Immediately move to exercise 2 (e.g., B2. Split Squat × 6 per leg)
3. Rest for the prescribed time
4. Repeat for the specified number of rounds

**Why these pairings work:**
- **Push + Lower:** Upper body push doesn't interfere with leg work
- **Pull + Push:** Opposite muscle groups can recover while working
- **Heavy + Isolation:** Main lift paired with smaller accessory movement
- **Lower + Upper Isolation:** Completely different body regions
- **Shoulder + Core:** Light isolation work that doesn't compete

**Standalone exercises:**
- Heavy compounds (Sumo DL, Pull-ups) need full rest
- Nordics are extremely demanding and need full recovery
- You can superset these if you want to save time, but quality may suffer

**Time savings:** Supersets cut workout time by ~30-40% compared to straight sets!

## 12-WEEK PROGRESSION PLAN

**Weeks 1-4: Build the Base**
- Focus on perfect form at current weights
- Add 1 rep per exercise per week
- Don't add weight yet

**Weeks 5-8: Add Volume**
- When you hit top of rep range (e.g., 3×12 instead of 3×8), add 5-10% weight
- Drop back to bottom of rep range and rebuild
- Nordics: Add assisted concentric if eccentrics are controlled

**Weeks 9-12: Push Standards**
- Target your goal numbers
- Expected progress: Dips 15-18 reps, Pull-ups 7-9 reps, most exercises near goal reps
- Sumo DL should be at 0.75-0.9×BW

**Weeks 13+: Hit Goals**
- Continue adding weight/reps every 1-2 weeks
- Should be at or very close to all standards

## EXERCISE REGRESSIONS (Use These If Needed)

**Dips:** Bench dips → Band-assisted dips → Full dips
**Pull-ups:** Negatives (5 sec) → Band-assisted → Full pull-ups
**Nordics:** 5-sec eccentric with hand assist → Full eccentric → Eccentric + concentric
**Split Squats:** Bodyweight → 10% BW → 25% BW
**RDL:** 60% BW → 80% BW → 100% BW

## GOAL STANDARDS (Where You're Heading)

- **Dips:** 20 reps (shoulders below elbows)
- **Nordic Curls:** 5 full reps (down and up)
- **External Rotation:** 12 reps @ 10% BW

- **Seated Calf Raise:** 15 reps @ 50% BW
- **Split Squat:** 8 reps @ 25% BW per hand
- **Pull-ups:** 10 reps
- **Romanian Deadlift:** 12 reps @ 100% BW
- **Shoulder Press:** 10 reps @ 25% BW per hand
- **Sumo Deadlift:** 1×BW
